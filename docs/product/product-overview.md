# Product Overview

## Vision
LeadFlow AI is a Windows-first workspace that helps service businesses discover relevant customer opportunities, understand intent with AI, and manage human-approved responses.

## Core promise
More relevant leads, less manual searching, faster review, safer outreach.

## Primary users
- Service business owners
- Sales/marketing operators
- Small growth teams

## Product loop
Discover → Filter → Deduplicate → Analyze → Score → Suggest → Review → Approve → Execute → Track

## Product principles
1. Human remains in control of outbound actions.
2. AI explains and assists; it does not silently act.
3. Every important action is auditable.
4. Provider integrations are isolated.
5. The UI prioritizes actionable lead information.
