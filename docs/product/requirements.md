# LeadFlow AI Product Requirements

## Goal

Help service businesses discover potential customers from supported Facebook/Meta sources, understand intent with AI, and manage responses through a human-in-the-loop workflow.

## Core workflow

Discover → Filter → Deduplicate → Score → Suggest → Review → Approve → Execute → Track

## MVP capabilities

- Campaign management
- Include/exclude keywords
- Lead Inbox
- Lead scoring
- AI-assisted intent analysis
- AI reply suggestions
- Human approval queue
- Audit log
- Provider adapter for official Meta integrations
- Pause/kill switch
- Rate limiting and deduplication

## Non-goals

- Credential/cookie scraping
- CAPTCHA bypass
- Stealth browser automation
- Unsolicited bulk outreach
- Hidden autonomous outbound actions

## UX requirement

The user must always be able to understand:
- where a lead came from
- why it received its score
- what AI recommends
- what action will happen
- whether an action is waiting for approval
- what happened after execution
